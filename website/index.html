QUESTIONS & INCLUSIVE DESIGN DETAILS

A few things you might be wondering.

Explore how SPARK LAB is designed around neurodivergent primary-school learners, overlapping learning differences, and the families and educators supporting them.

Who are we designing for?

SPARK LAB is designed first and foremost for primary-school learners (ages 6–11) with ADHD, who often experience executive-function overload, working-memory fatigue, time blindness, or sensory overwhelm when faced with traditional, text-heavy school worksheets. Because neurodivergent learning profiles frequently overlap, our architecture also embeds multi-sensory accessibility for co-occurring learning differences alongside dedicated tools for the adults cheering them on:

PRIMARY LEARNERS WITH ADHD

Executive Function & Pacing Support

Replaces intimidating walls of instructions with the AI Task Atomizer—breaking assignments into a two-minute starter and one doable micro-step at a time. Energy check-ins and a no-shame pause button let children regulate their focus without losing their place or confidence.

DYSLEXIA, DYSGRAPHIA & DYSCALCULIA

Multi-Sensory Learning Pathways

Supports dyslexia with adjustable text sizing, generous spacing, warm low-glare backgrounds, and follow-along read-aloud speech (TTS); dysgraphia with low-friction sentence starters and spoken idea prompts; and dyscalculia with visual 3D maths blocks that make abstract quantities concrete.

PARENTS & CAREGIVERS

Calmer Homework & Shared Milestones

Provides families with a pressure-free companion view that translates school tasks into gentle home routines, highlights small daily wins, and helps parents understand how their child’s energy and focus shift across the week.

EDUCATORS & SENCOs

Practical Classroom Differentiation

Equips primary-school teachers and learning support coordinators with inclusive assignment templates, sensory-safe display presets, and clear visibility into where a student paused—so support feels timely and encouraging.

Is SPARK LAB ready to use?

SPARK LAB is currently an interactive concept demonstration and research-informed web prototype built by our five-member student Scrum Team. You can try our core interaction concepts directly on this page today while the full platform is developed across iterative Sprints:

  • Live in this browser demo: Try the Small Steps & Energy Check-In task flow, test the Read-Aloud Story Reader with live font scaling and browser voice synthesis, and interact with our 3D Spark Study-Buddy sculptures.
  • In active Sprint development: Automated AI task breakdown for custom classroom worksheets, persistent learner workspaces, interactive visual maths manipulatives, and connected parent/educator dashboards.
How does SPARK LAB adapt to a child’s daily energy and focus?

For children with ADHD, attention and executive readiness aren’t the same every day—trying harder on a depleted afternoon often leads to frustration. SPARK LAB begins every session with an Energy Check-In that reshapes the workspace immediately:

  • “A little” energy (Low-Demand Mode): Hides future steps and narrows the screen to a single, gentle 1-minute starter so starting feels safe and achievable.
  • “Some” energy (Guided Flow): Reveals a structured three-step checklist with a calm progress bar and encouraging micro-feedback after every completed step.
  • “Lots” of energy (Discovery Mode): Encourages deeper creative exploration across reading, audio storytelling, and interactive 3D models.
  • No-Shame Pause: Learners can tap “I need a pause” at any time for a grounding shoulder-relaxation prompt without penalties or countdown timers.
How is the SPARK LAB Scrum Team organized?

We work as a cross-functional, self-managing five-member Scrum Team following the official Scrum Guide accountabilities—uniting product vision, agile coaching, and full-stack software engineering:

  • Product Owner (Dunith Peiris): Establishes the Product Goal, orders the Product Backlog around neurodivergent learner outcomes, and leads inclusive UI/UX and sensory wellness design.
  • Scrum Master (Sanithu Kudaliyana): Coaches the team in empirical Scrum practices, facilitates Sprint events, removes impediments, and oversees system architecture, quality assurance, and accessibility testing.
  • Developers (Navindu Fernando, Vanuja Liyanage & Yohan Singappulige): Collaboratively plan, engineer, test, and integrate usable Sprint Increments across full-stack web architecture, the adaptive Task Atomizer engine, multi-modal TTS speech, and interactive 3D WebGL scenes.
Can I turn off the visual effects and customize sensory comfort?

Yes. Sensory regulation is built directly into SPARK LAB’s interface standards so visual motion never competes with a child’s concentration:

  • Instant Reduced-Motion Toggle: Check the “Reduce motion and depth effects” box in the interactive demo section to immediately turn off 3D card tilt, smooth scroll transitions, and continuous 3D rotation.
  • Automatic Device Sync: The platform automatically detects and respects your operating system’s prefers-reduced-motion accessibility setting as soon as the page loads.
  • Low-Stimulation Visuals: All surfaces use soft pastel contrast, generous whitespace, and zero flashing, strobing, or rapid animations.
Does this demo collect personal information or student data?

This demonstration has no student accounts, analytics, or forms that collect learning records. Hosting providers may process technical access logs. Because we design for primary-school learners, strict privacy protection comes first:

  • Local-Only Session State: Energy check-in choices, completed story steps, and text-size adjustments stay inside your current browser tab and reset automatically when you refresh or leave the page.
  • On-Device Read-Aloud Voice: The reader uses browser-provided speech synthesis. Voice availability and whether processing is local or online depend on your browser and selected voice. This page does not record your voice.
  • Transparent Visuals: The robot and family illustrations are AI-generated concept artwork, while the Scrum Team section features our real university project contributors.

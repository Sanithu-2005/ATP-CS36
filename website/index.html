A DIFFERENT STARTING POINT

Different minds.
Not different potential.

When a task feels too big, a page feels too busy, or focus slips away, trying harder isn't always the answer.

SPARK LAB is a proposed learning platform for primary-school children with ADHD, with support ideas for reading, writing and maths differences. We're designing the learning experience to adapt to the learner.

BEHIND EVERY LITTLE WIN

More than a lesson.
A moment of
“I can do this.”

We’re imagining learning days with less pressure and more discovery. Where a small start matters. Where taking a break is okay. Where different ways of learning belong.

Smaller steps, more ways to explore, and a learning space you can make your own. Instead of facing a wall of instructions, children focus on one small idea at a time and explore swipeable learning cards, visual maths blocks and short recall quizzes at their own pace.

A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Curious about the details?

What’s included, how the demo works, and what comes next.

Who is Spark Lab designed for?

Our project focuses on primary-school learners with ADHD and the families and educators supporting them. Small lessons, visual explanations, readable text and a calm pause space are central to the design.

What can I try on this page?

The browser prototype includes a Micro-Learning Feed with swipeable cards, Visual Math Blocks (US7), Active Recall Quizzes and Badges (US4), a Panic Button (US9), and an Educator Dashboard (US15). The Spark companion remains available below the learning lab.

How do learning cards and badges work?

Browse four sample cards and mark each as learned. Complete one card for First Spark and all four for Curious Mind. A perfect score on the three-question recall quiz earns Recall Star. Repeat answers do not add points, and retrying a quiz does not remove badges you already earned.

What happens when I press the panic button?

The “I need a pause” button opens a quiet screen and pauses companion motion. You can stay as long as you need, then return to the same activity. Your cards, quiz answers and badges remain unchanged. It is a learning comfort control, not an emergency service.

How does demo sign-in work?

Choose a learner or educator sample account on the sign-in screen, then select Sign in to demo. Learners arrive at the learning cards; educators arrive at the fictional classroom dashboard. These are local demonstration roles, not secure accounts. You can switch roles, sign out, or browse the learner activities as a guest.

What does the educator dashboard show?

The dashboard combines three fictional learners with your current browser session. It shows completed cards, latest quiz results and an activity filter. Select View for badge counts and pause information. It does not connect to real classrooms, send alerts or store student records.

Does the demo save learner data?

The sign-in screen uses two public sample accounts. There is no real authentication, account registration or connected student-record system. Demo sign-in, progress, badges and text-size preferences live only in the current tab and reset when the page reloads. Hosting providers may process technical access logs. The robot and family scenes are AI-created concept artwork.

Is the full product available to download?

The full product is still in development. These working browser interactions demonstrate the intended experience; a downloadable learning application is not yet available.
